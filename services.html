Services

Ways we can work together

Every new client starts with a 60-minute intake so we can set goals and decide on a plan.

Individual therapy

53-minute sessions · weekly or every other week

For adults 18 and older. Focused on anxiety, depression, grief, transitions, and burnout.

Couples therapy

80-minute sessions · weekly to start

For couples who keep having the same argument, feel distant, or are recovering from a rupture.

Telehealth

Secure video · clients located in Georgia

The same care from home, using a HIPAA-compliant video platform. No downloads needed.

Free consultation

15 minutes · by phone

A no-pressure call to see if we're a good fit. If we're not, I'll suggest someone who might be.
