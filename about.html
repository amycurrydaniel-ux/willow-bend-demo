About

Hi, I'm Dana.

I've been a counselor for twelve years, first in community mental health and now in private practice. My style is warm and direct. I'll listen closely, ask honest questions, and give you practical tools you can use between sessions.

I draw mainly on cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), acceptance and commitment therapy (ACT), and the Gottman Method for couples. I tailor the approach to you rather than fitting you to a method.

Outside the office, I garden badly and walk the PATH trail most mornings.
